# UK Passport Photo Specification

Use this file as the local contract for SnapPass photo analysis and cleanup agents when UK passport is selected.

## Output

- Printable paper photo target: 35 x 45 mm.
- Digital output should be at least 600 x 750 pixels when using the same aspect ratio.

## Subject

- Exactly one human subject.
- Subject must face the camera directly.
- Eyes should be open and visible.
- Expression should be neutral.
- Do not identify the person or infer sensitive attributes.

## Composition

- Head must be centered.
- Head height should fit the 35 x 45 mm document-photo frame, commonly around 29-34 mm.
- Full face must be visible with no crop through hair, chin, or face outline.

## Background

- Background should be plain and light-colored.
- No shadows, texture, objects, other people, or visible room details.

## Lighting And Quality

- Lighting should be even across the face.
- Avoid red eye, glare, shadows, blur, filters, and heavy retouching.
- Preserve original facial features, identity, skin texture, hairline, expression, head shape, clothing, and pose.

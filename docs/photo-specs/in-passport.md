# India Passport Photo Specification

Use this file as the local contract for SnapPass photo analysis and cleanup agents when India passport is selected.

## Output

- Digital output should be square.
- Recommended digital size: at least 600 x 600 pixels.
- Printable paper photo target: 51 x 51 mm.
- Printable 4x6 sheet target: four 51 x 51 mm photos plus blank quote space when space allows.

## Subject

- Exactly one human subject.
- Subject must face the camera directly.
- Full face must be visible.
- Eyes should be open and visible.
- Expression should be neutral or natural.
- Do not identify the person or infer sensitive attributes.

## Composition

- Head must be centered horizontally.
- Full face, including chin and top of head or hair, should fit inside the square crop.
- Keep the face large enough for document review without cropping hair, chin, or shoulders too tightly.

## Background

- Background should be plain white.
- No shadows, texture, objects, other people, or visible room details.
- Background replacement may alter only the background.

## Lighting And Quality

- Lighting should be even across the face.
- Avoid hard shadows, glare, overexposure, underexposure, blur, filters, and heavy retouching.
- Preserve original facial features, identity, skin texture, hairline, expression, head shape, clothing, and pose.

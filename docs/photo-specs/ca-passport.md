# Canada Passport Photo Specification

Use this file as the local contract for SnapPass photo analysis and cleanup agents when Canada passport is selected.

## Output

- Printable paper photo target: 50 x 70 mm.
- Digital preview target: 600 x 840 pixels or higher at the same aspect ratio.

## Subject

- Exactly one human subject.
- Subject must face the camera directly.
- Eyes should be open and visible.
- Expression should be neutral.
- Do not identify the person or infer sensitive attributes.

## Composition

- Face must be centered.
- Face height should be approximately 31-36 mm from chin to crown of head in the printed photo.
- Leave clear space around the head and shoulders.

## Background

- Background should be plain white or light-colored.
- No shadows, texture, objects, other people, or visible room details.

## Lighting And Quality

- Lighting should be even across the face and background.
- Avoid glare, shadows, blur, filters, and heavy retouching.
- Preserve original facial features, identity, skin texture, hairline, expression, head shape, clothing, and pose.

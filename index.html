Print pickup roadmap

Retail print ordering should be a server-side handoff.

Walgreens is the cleanest first partner when API access is approved: SnapPass can generate the 4x6 sheet, send a temporary image URL, store choice, and contact details, then show the returned order ID. For Walmart, CVS, and other retailers, integrations often route through print platforms such as PNI Digital Media or Fujifilm Imagine rather than direct public APIs.

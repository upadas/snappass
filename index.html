Photo requirements

2026 US passport photo checklist

Quick examples for what SnapPass checks before it creates the final 600 x 600 digital photo or printable 4x6 sheet.

Composition & lightingRead more

Use even front lighting with no harsh shadows. Keep the full face visible and centered inside the square crop.

Pose & expressionRead more

Face the camera directly with a neutral expression. Do not tilt, turn sideways, or use a selfie angle.

Clothing & accessoriesRead more

Avoid uniforms, hats, heavy glare, and anything that hides the face. Religious head coverings are okay when the full face is visible.

Children & babiesRead more

Only the child should be visible. Keep eyes open when possible, use a plain background, and avoid hands or toys in frame.

Technical specificationsRead more

Use a square image. US digital output should be at least 600 x 600 px with no filters or heavy retouching.

Submission readinessRead more

Before downloading, confirm the background, head size, eye height, and crop all pass the live preview checks.

Print pickup roadmap

Retail print ordering should be a server-side handoff.

Walgreens is the cleanest first partner when API access is approved: SnapPass can generate the 4x6 sheet, send a temporary image URL, store choice, and contact details, then show the returned order ID. A generic print handoff API can also support Walmart, CVS, and other retailers through providers such as PNI Digital Media or Fujifilm Imagine when direct public APIs are limited.
